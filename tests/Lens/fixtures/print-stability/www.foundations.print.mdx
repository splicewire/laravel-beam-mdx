# Foundations

These pages explain the ideas the rest of the Beam reference takes for granted. Read them in order the first time; after that, each one stands alone.

- [The layers](/beam/docs/foundations/layers): the three vendor names and which way references point.
- [Popcorn registries](/beam/docs/foundations/registries): roots, entries and owners, and why the reference is addressed by registry root.
- [The contract pivot](/beam/docs/foundations/contract-pivot): how declared data becomes the contract between producer and consumer.
- [Particles](/beam/docs/foundations/particles): the one shape every boundary-crossing value takes.
- [Realms and surfaces](/beam/docs/foundations/realms-and-surfaces): who a page is for, and where it appears.
- [What is not a registry](/beam/docs/foundations/not-a-registry): bindings, routes, gates and the other mechanisms that look like registries and are not.

The [registry reference](/beam/docs/registries) documents every registry the product declares, one page per root.
